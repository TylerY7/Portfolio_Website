    Hello! My name is Tyler Young, and I am a senior Computer Science major at Valdosta State University, on track to graduate in Summer 2025. Over the past four years, I’ve actively engaged with my department and community—serving as the sole student ambassador for the Computer Science program and leading as president of VSU’s chapter of the Association for Computing Machinery (ACM).

This interest was solidified during my internship as a Software Engineering (SWEG) Intern at Warner Robins Air Force Base in Warner Robins, GA, where I gained hands-on experience in a mission-driven environment. I am eager to continue applying my skills and expanding my impact after graduation.
